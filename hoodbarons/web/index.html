How this is computed

Per strike: Black-Scholes gamma × open interest × 100 × spot² × 1%. Calls count positive (dealers long), puts negative (dealers short). The gamma flip is where net dealer gamma crosses zero; the walls are the largest positive and negative strikes. Chain data is delayed about 15 minutes. real-time coming soon
